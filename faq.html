AAbout SMAYAA.ai – Industrial Water Intelligence

01What is SMAYAA.ai?

SMAYAA.ai is an AI-powered industrial water intelligence platform designed to optimize cooling water systems, reduce freshwater consumption, improve cooling efficiency, and enhance equipment reliability. It combines machine learning, predictive analytics, and engineering intelligence to transform industrial water data into actionable operational recommendations.

02How does SMAYAA.ai work?

SMAYAA collects operational data from existing sensors, PLCs, SCADA, and industrial automation systems. It processes water quality, flow, temperature, pressure, and equipment performance data using engineering calculations and intelligent analytics to identify inefficiencies, detect abnormalities, predict potential risks, and recommend corrective actions.

03Why do industries need AI-based water management?

Industries face increasing pressure to conserve freshwater, control energy costs, improve recycling, and avoid production interruptions. AI-based water management helps identify interconnected problems that conventional monitoring may not reveal, supporting better operating decisions and improved industrial efficiency.

04What makes SMAYAA different from conventional SCADA systems?

SCADA systems primarily monitor and supervise industrial processes. SMAYAA complements existing automation infrastructure by adding engineering analysis, multivariable correlation, predictive intelligence, and optimization recommendations to help operators understand why problems occur and how operating performance may be improved.

05Does SMAYAA replace existing PLCs, SCADA, or automation systems?

No. SMAYAA is designed to integrate with existing industrial control and monitoring infrastructure. It adds an intelligent decision-support layer without requiring the replacement of established PLC, SCADA, or supervisory systems.

BCooling Water Optimization and Industrial Applications

06How can AI optimize industrial cooling water systems?

AI-based cooling water optimization evaluates operating data such as water temperatures, flow rates, conductivity, water chemistry, filtration performance, and cooling tower efficiency. By analyzing the relationships between these parameters, SMAYAA is designed to identify operating inefficiencies and recommend improvements in water, energy, and cooling performance.

07How does SMAYAA help reduce freshwater consumption?

SMAYAA analyzes make-up water, evaporation, blowdown, water losses, and cycles of concentration to identify opportunities for water conservation. Its water balance intelligence can help operators detect unexplained losses, evaluate blowdown requirements, and improve water reuse while respecting operating constraints.

08What industries can benefit from SMAYAA?

SMAYAA is designed for water-intensive operations, including steel, power generation, pharmaceuticals, paper, cement, refineries, petrochemicals, data centres, HVAC, and district cooling. Its water quality intelligence capabilities may also be extended to municipal water treatment, tertiary water reuse, irrigation, aquaculture, and other applications.

09How can SMAYAA improve water management in steel plants?

Steel plants face challenges involving high suspended solids, oil contamination, scaling, corrosion, equipment fouling, and fluctuating thermal loads. SMAYAA can correlate cooling water quality, filtration performance, water chemistry, and thermal operating conditions to help identify problems that may threaten rolling mills, casting systems, heat exchangers, and other critical equipment.

10How can SMAYAA optimize cooling water in power plants?

SMAYAA can evaluate cooling tower performance, condenser cooling conditions, water consumption, cycles of concentration, filtration efficiency, and water chemistry. These insights can support improved thermal efficiency, more effective water management, reduced fouling risk, and better equipment reliability.

11Can SMAYAA be used in data centres and HVAC cooling systems?

Yes. SMAYAA's cooling water intelligence approach is relevant to water-cooled data centres, commercial HVAC systems, and district cooling facilities. It can help analyze cooling water consumption, thermal performance, water quality, and potential energy-efficiency improvements, subject to the available instrumentation and system integration.

12Can SMAYAA optimize tertiary-treated wastewater reuse?

SMAYAA can support the monitoring and optimization of tertiary-treated water used in industrial cooling and other reuse applications. It can correlate water quality, conductivity, suspended solids, filtration performance, and chemistry to help operators manage reuse water consistently and identify conditions that may affect equipment reliability.

CWater Chemistry, Filtration and Predictive Maintenance

13How can SMAYAA help prevent scaling and corrosion?

SMAYAA evaluates water chemistry and relevant operating parameters, including pH, conductivity, hardness, alkalinity, temperature, and scaling or corrosion indicators. Its intelligence modules are designed to identify adverse trends early and support recommendations for improved water treatment and equipment protection.

14Can SMAYAA detect oil contamination in cooling water?

Where suitable oil-in-water sensors or laboratory measurements are available, SMAYAA can analyze oil contamination data alongside turbidity, differential pressure, and thermal performance. This can help identify relationships between contamination events, filter fouling, and deteriorating cooling system performance.

15How can SMAYAA identify high dirt loads, fly ash and suspended solids?

SMAYAA can evaluate turbidity, total suspended solids, filtration differential pressure, and cleaning cycles to identify abnormal solids loading and filtration problems. This is particularly relevant to steel plants, power stations, and industrial water systems operating under challenging contamination conditions.

16Can SMAYAA predict microbiological fouling and Legionella risk?

SMAYAA is designed to evaluate operating conditions associated with microbial growth and biofouling, including water temperature, chemistry, stagnation indicators, and treatment performance. These insights may support early identification of elevated risk conditions. However, Legionella detection requires appropriate microbiological testing, and SMAYAA does not replace established cooling tower water safety programmes.

17How does SMAYAA help reduce unexpected production downtime?

SMAYAA analyzes relationships between water quality, cooling performance, filtration, and equipment operating conditions. By identifying abnormal patterns and emerging risks, the platform is designed to support preventive actions before water-related issues develop into equipment failures or production interruptions.

18What is predictive maintenance in industrial water systems?

Predictive maintenance uses equipment data, operating trends, engineering calculations, and machine learning to identify conditions associated with deterioration or failure. In cooling water applications, this may include abnormal filter pressure, reduced heat transfer, scaling indicators, and unusual cleaning cycles.

DEngineering Intelligence and AI Modules

19What are the main intelligence modules in SMAYAA?

SMAYAA's modular architecture covers cooling tower thermal performance, water balance, cycles of concentration, water chemistry, scaling and corrosion indicators, filtration, biological fouling risk, energy performance, anomaly detection, and multi-objective optimization. Module availability and deployment readiness may vary.

20What is macro water balance, and why is it important?

Macro water balance is the analysis of water entering, circulating within, and leaving an industrial water system. It includes make-up water, evaporation, blowdown, drift, backwash, other losses, and changes in storage. A reliable water balance helps identify unaccounted water and opportunities for conservation.

21What is cycles of concentration (COC) optimization?

Cycles of concentration represent the concentration of dissolved substances in recirculating cooling water relative to make-up water, commonly estimated using conductivity. Optimizing COC can help reduce unnecessary blowdown and freshwater demand while maintaining acceptable scaling, corrosion, and microbiological control conditions.

22How does machine learning improve cooling tower performance?

Machine learning can identify relationships between cooling tower operating parameters such as wet-bulb temperature, cooling range, approach, heat load, water chemistry, and fan operation. SMAYAA aims to use these relationships to identify abnormal performance and recommend more efficient operating conditions.

23Can SMAYAA integrate with Siemens, Allen-Bradley and Rockwell Automation systems?

SMAYAA is designed to work with existing industrial automation infrastructure, including systems based on Siemens and Rockwell Automation technologies, such as Allen-Bradley PLCs. Integration depends on the installed hardware, available communication protocols, system configuration, and data accessibility.

24What is the SMAYAA Edge Intelligence Box?

The SMAYAA Edge Intelligence Box is the proposed interface between plant instrumentation and the intelligence platform. Its architecture includes sensor and PLC data collection, validation, engineering calculations, KPI generation, and communication with cloud-based intelligence modules. Additional on-device machine learning capabilities are being evaluated.
