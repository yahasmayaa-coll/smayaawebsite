Problem To Prediction

Real industrial problems that inspired SMAYAA

Scaling & heat transfer loss

Forecasts scaling tendency from water chemistry and ΔT drift, before heat transfer drops.

Corrosion & asset damage

Tracks corrosion risk across metallurgy so inhibitor dosing matches real conditions.

Oil in cooling water

Flags hydrocarbon ingress early from conductivity, turbidity and process signals.

Biofouling & microbiological growth

Spots the conditions that favour microbial growth and times biocide dosing to them.

High dirt loads & clogging

Predicts filter and strainer loading so cleaning is planned, not an emergency.

Deposit build-up in scale pits

Models where solids settle so pit cleaning happens before capacity is lost.
