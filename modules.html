01TH-01

Cooling Tower Thermal Intelligence

Monitor cooling tower range, approach, wet-bulb temperature, heat load, and cooling performance to identify efficiency losses and opportunities for improvement.

02WB-01

Water Balance Intelligence

Track make-up water, evaporation, blowdown, drift, and other water losses to identify excessive water consumption and opportunities for freshwater savings.

03COC-01

Cycles of Concentration Optimization

Analyze conductivity, make-up water quality, blowdown, and operating constraints to recommend optimized cycles of concentration and reduced water wastage.

04

Water Chemistry & Scaling Intelligence

Monitor water chemistry, hardness, alkalinity, pH, conductivity, and scaling or corrosion indicators to assess risks to cooling equipment and heat transfer surfaces.

05FLT

Filtration Intelligence

Analyze filtration performance, turbidity, suspended solids, differential pressure, cleaning cycles, and filter efficiency to identify fouling and optimize filtration operations.

06BIO

Biological Fouling Intelligence

Evaluate available microbiological and operating data to identify conditions that increase biofouling risk and support preventive maintenance.

07

Heat Transfer & Energy Intelligence

Correlate cooling performance, water quality, flow, temperature, fan operation, and pumping energy to identify opportunities for improved thermal and energy efficiency.

08

Predictive Maintenance & Anomaly Detection

Use historical trends, multivariable analysis, and machine learning to identify unusual operating conditions and emerging equipment-performance problems before they cause major disruptions.

09CTRL-01

Multi-Objective Optimization Intelligence

Evaluate the relationship between water consumption, cooling efficiency, energy, chemical consumption, and equipment reliability to recommend technically feasible operating improvements.

10

Engineering Intelligence & Performance Validation

Perform engineering calculations, sensor-data validation, KPI monitoring, baseline comparisons, and performance verification to support reliable operational recommendations.
