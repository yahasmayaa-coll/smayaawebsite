About the system

Water filtration systems have long required a unified platform that enables data mining and dashboarding to predict, analyse and report on trends. SMAYAA serves as a complementary intelligent platform without replacing existing supervisory systems. Its goal is to ensure high quality of water, protection of assets and a minimum to nil disruption in production and/or quality of marine life in aquariums, cultivation units and aqua culture farming.
